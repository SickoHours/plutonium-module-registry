# Rights and removal

Every entry points at a repository someone else owns. The submitter confirms, when listing, that
the repository is public, that its license is documented, and that they hold the rights to every
asset in it or have listed the module as `distribution: private` with no such asset in the tree.
This registry hosts no game data and redistributes nothing.

If a listing points at material you own and did not license, open a **Rights or removal**
issue naming the entry and the material, or use private vulnerability reporting if the matter is
sensitive. A maintainer removes the listing and records the removal; the entry name is retired
and never reused.
